Questions

Before you light it.

What exactly am I buying?

One window in this tower, permanently. It shows the colour and mark you choose, opens your link when someone clicks it, and shows your name and tagline on hover.

How long until it's lit?

Seconds. Payment is confirmed on our server by Stripe, and the window turns on without anyone approving it by hand.

Can I change my link later?

Yes. Write to us through GB Solutions with your floor and unit, and we'll update it.

What if two people take the same window?

It can't stick. Windows are locked as they sell, and if a rare collision does occur that window is refunded automatically.

Who runs the tower?

GB Solutions Oy, a company in Finland. Payments are handled by Stripe — card details are never entered on this page.

About

Plots is a single digital building shared by everyone who opens this page. Six hundred windows, one payment each, and a light that stays on. It isn't advertising space that resets every month — it's a place with an address, kept by whoever lit it first.

The tower is original and fictional: an Art-Deco silhouette with a stepped crown and an illuminated spire, standing in a city that exists only here. Your window is a real coordinate inside it.
